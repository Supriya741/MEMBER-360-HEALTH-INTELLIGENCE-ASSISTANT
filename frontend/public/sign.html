Privacy Policy

Member 360° Health Intelligence Assistant • v4.2.0

HIPAA & SOC 2 Compliance Guarantee: Member 360° is committed to rigorous data protection standards. Patient data is encrypted with military-grade AES-256 and is never used to train public AI models.

1. Scope & Protected Health Information (PHI)

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of clinical analytics, patient encounter metadata, and user interactions within the Member 360° Health Intelligence Assistant. All PHI handling strictly complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), HITECH, and executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).

2. Artificial Intelligence & Assistant Privacy

The Member 360° Health Intelligence Assistant operates under strict ethical AI principles:

  • Tenant Isolation: Queries and clinical analyses are processed in single-tenant, isolated virtual private environments.
  • No Public Model Training: Your clinical data, notes, and search prompts are never utilized to train external public foundation models.
  • Tamper-Evident Auditing: All clinical suggestions and queries are recorded with immutable timestamps for compliance audits.

3. Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy enterprise defense-in-depth protocols:

  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 encryption applied across all databases, file stores, and backups.
  • Encryption in Transit: Enforced TLS 1.3 encryption across all API gateways and web sockets.
  • Access Governance: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) prevent unauthorized access.

4. Data Collection & Purpose

Information processed includes institutional credentials (email, name, role), clinical metrics for risk stratification, care gap identification, and diagnostic support indices.

5. Governance & Inquiries

For data privacy queries or audit log export requests, contact the Data Protection Officer at privacy@member360health.org.

Clinical Decision Support Disclaimer: Member 360° Health Intelligence Assistant is designed to augment healthcare providers. It is not an autonomous diagnostic device. All final clinical decisions and treatment plans remain the sole responsibility of licensed attending clinicians.

1. Authorized Institutional Access

Access is strictly reserved for authorized clinicians and administrators affiliated with contracted healthcare organizations. Authentication must occur via approved institutional single sign-on (SSO) or verified credentials.

2. User Obligations & Credential Confidentiality

  • Individual Responsibility: Users are strictly prohibited from sharing login credentials, session tokens, or MFA codes.
  • Session Security: Clinical workstations must be locked when left unattended.
  • Permitted Use: The assistant must only be queried for authorized clinical decision support, population health management, and operational analytics.

3. Assistant & Analytics Utilization

AI-synthesized summaries, risk calculations, and care suggestions must be reviewed and validated by a qualified practitioner prior to taking clinical action.
